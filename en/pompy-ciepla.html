The right design

We do not start with the equipment brand.

What matters most is how the system will perform in a specific home. Incorrect capacity or an excessively high flow temperature can increase costs and reduce comfort.

  • We calculate actual heat demand instead of relying on floor area alone.
  • We check radiators, underfloor heating, the buffer tank and the domestic hot-water cylinder.
  • We plan the outdoor unit location with noise and defrosting in mind.
  • We present an economical, recommended and extended option.

FAQ

Questions about heat pumps.

We assess the conditions first, then provide a specific answer.

Will a heat pump work in an older home?

It can work, but the building's heat loss, flow temperature and system condition must be assessed. In some cases, partial thermal modernisation is the better first step.

How do you select the equipment capacity?

We consider the building's heat demand, insulation, heat distribution system, domestic hot-water preparation and local conditions. Floor area alone is not enough.

Can a heat pump work with radiators?

Yes, provided the radiator parameters and flow temperature can deliver the required comfort. We verify this before selecting the unit.

Do you help with grants and financing?

Yes. We check current programmes and eligibility rules and help with the documentation. Funding availability is confirmed individually.
