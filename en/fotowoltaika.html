Capacity selection

The consumption profile matters, not only the annual total.

Two households with the same bill may need different solutions. What matters is when energy is used and whether new loads will be added soon.

  • We analyse previous consumption and planned changes in the home or business.
  • We check orientation, angle, shading and the load-bearing capacity of the installation area.
  • We account for integration with a heat pump, wallbox and battery storage.
  • We compare a basic, recommended and expansion-ready option.

FAQ

Questions about photovoltaics.

We base a specific answer on your bills and installation conditions.

How should system capacity be selected?

We analyse annual and hourly consumption, installation conditions, shading and planned new loads such as a heat pump or electric car.

Does photovoltaics work in winter?

Yes, but it produces less energy due to shorter days and lower solar irradiation. Performance should be assessed over the whole year.

Can battery storage be added later?

Usually yes, but the scope depends on the inverter, electrical installation and selected system. It is worth planning for expansion at the design stage.

Do you handle the paperwork?

We prepare the documentation included in the agreed scope and explain the next steps for grid connection and any financing.
