Frequently asked questions

Clear answers, without the technical fog.

We answer detailed questions after analysing the building — that is when the answer has real value.

Do you install systems throughout Poland?

Yes. We serve projects throughout Poland. We confirm appointment availability and any travel costs before preparing the final quotation.

How long does it take to prepare a quotation?

We usually provide an initial scope and price range within 24–48 hours after receiving all required information. Complex projects require an audit.

Do you help customers apply for grants?

Yes. We check whether the project and applicant meet the current programme requirements and then assist with the documentation. The final decision always rests with the institution awarding the support.

Do you service systems installed by other companies?

Yes, following an initial diagnosis and a check of spare-part availability and equipment documentation.

Why do prices vary?

The price is affected by capacity, manufacturer, equipment standard, cable and pipe runs, roof construction, electrical upgrades and the scope of protection equipment. That is why we show indicative prices and prepare three proposal options.

Are the prices shown on the website final?

No. They are indicative gross market prices for a typical scope. We provide a binding quotation only after collecting the technical information.
