Backup power

Backup must be designed, not simply switched on.

Having a battery does not automatically mean the whole home will operate during an outage. We set priorities and design the system for actual loads.

  • We select critical circuits, such as the refrigerator, lighting, internet, controls and heating source.
  • We check appliance running and start-up power and estimate possible backup duration.
  • We select the inverter, transfer controls and required protection devices.
  • We test a grid-outage scenario during commissioning.

FAQ

Questions about battery storage.

The most important answers depend on consumption data and the existing installation.

How should storage capacity be selected?

We analyse PV generation, hourly consumption, the desired self-consumption level and the appliances that should operate during an outage.

Can the battery power the entire home during an outage?

Only if the system, inverter, battery power and electrical installation were designed for it. We often separate the most important circuits to extend runtime.

Can a battery be added to an existing PV system?

In many systems, yes. We first check the inverter, protection devices, grid arrangement and possible integration options.

How long will appliances run on the battery?

It depends on usable capacity, current consumption and appliance power. Once the circuits are identified, we can prepare an indicative backup-runtime scenario.
